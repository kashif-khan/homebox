Homebox can track when a consumable expires and tell you about it at the times *you* choose: a month before, a week before,
the day itself, a week after.

## Expiry dates

Every item has an optional **Expires** date on its edit page, with a type: *Expires*, *Use by* or *Best before*. The item
page shows how far away it is ("Best before · Expires in 4 days"), the item list has an Expires column (turn it on in the
column picker) and a search filter, and the home page gets an **Expired / Expiring soon** card when anything needs attention.

You can sort the list by expiry; items without a date always sort last.

### Make it automatic

- **Templates.** In a template, tick *Set an expiry date on new items* and give a number of days. A "Milk" template with
  `7` days sets the date to a week from the day you add the item.
- **Entity types.** Tick *Items of this type must have an expiry date* on a type such as "Medicine". Saving an item of that
  type without a date is then refused.
- **CSV.** `HB.expires_at` (`YYYY-MM-DD`) and `HB.expiry_kind` columns are imported and exported.

<Aside>
The date is a calendar day. "Today" for counting days is the collection's today, in the collection's time zone (see
*Collection → Settings*), not the server's or your browser's.
</Aside>

## Alert rules

Under *Collection → Alerts* you create rules. A rule says:

- **What to watch:** an item's expiry, a warranty ending, or maintenance coming due.
- **When:** any number of offsets: `30 days before`, `7 days before`, `1 day before`, `On the day`, `7 days after`.
- **Which items:** all, or only one entity type and/or tag. Medicine can alert a month ahead while tinned food alerts a week
  ahead.
- **Who is told:** chosen notifiers, or every active one if you pick none.

Alerts go out as **one digest per notifier**, grouped into expiry, warranty and maintenance sections, soonest first, with the
item's location.

### When they go out

Each collection has a **time zone** and a **send hour** (default 08:00). A day's alerts go out from that hour, local time, not
the server's. Homebox checks every few minutes, so an alert is never more than a few minutes late.

If Homebox was **down** at the time, the alert is **sent late rather than dropped**, as soon as it is back, for up to 7 days
after it was due. After that it is considered stale and skipped, so a long outage or a brand-new rule never floods you with
old news. Each alert goes out **once** per notifier and offset: it is remembered, and if you correct an item's date it counts
as a new alert for the new date.

If a notifier fails (the service is down), nothing is recorded and the next check tries again.

### Existing maintenance reminders

Maintenance reminders keep working. On upgrade, every collection that already had notifiers gets a **Maintenance due** rule
(on the day, to every active notifier) - the same reminder as before. You can edit or delete it like any other rule; once
deleted it stays deleted.

## Time zones and your account

When you register, Homebox pre-fills your country, currency and time zone from your browser. They are only suggestions: change
anything that is wrong on the sign-up form, your profile, or *Collection → Settings*. Nothing is looked up from your IP
address.

## Webhooks

Date alerts can also call other systems: see [Webhooks](/en/advanced/webhooks), which turn the same rules into
`expiry.upcoming`, `expiry.expired`, `warranty.upcoming` and similar events.
